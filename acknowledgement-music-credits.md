# Acknowledgement music

The active clip, `acknowledgement-nicholas-tribute.mp3`, combines the user's spoken dedication with a **16.8-second opening main-theme excerpt of Hava Nagila**. The instrumental level automatically drops under the speech.

The dedication is synthesized from the user's existing voice model. The user confirmed that the model represents their own voice performing an accent/impression.
