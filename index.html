I would also like to thank my friends and colleagues at the NeuroGrad programme and at NRU, especially Miriam, for your friendship, curiosity, and for the many conversations we have had about science, life, and everything around the edges. I am grateful for the many small detours that made these years much richer. I would also like to thank Nicholas for your spontaneous company during my exchange, it was a very warm welcome, the memories of which mean a lot to me. A very special thank you to Rikke for your friendship and support. You have become family to me, and I am so grateful for you.

Finally, to Clara, thank you for your love, humour, and support throughout this project. Of all the things these years have brought me, meeting you has meant the most.
